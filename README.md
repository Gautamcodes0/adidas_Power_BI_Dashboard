# adidas_Power_BI_Dashboard
Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard 📊 A Power BI dashboard visualizing Adidas sales performance . It highlights total sales, profit, units sold, and price per unit, with insights by month, state, region, product, and retailer to track growth and identify top-performing areas and products.

🏷️ Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Adidas sales data (2020–2021). The goal is to uncover sales performance trends, regional contributions, and top-performing products and retailers. The report helps business stakeholders make data-driven decisions to improve revenue, marketing, and inventory management.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze total sales, operating profit, and margin.

Track monthly sales trends and performance growth.

Identify top-performing states and regions in the U.S.

Compare product category and retailer performance.

Provide actionable insights for strategic business planning.

🧩 Key Insights

Total Sales: $892.66M

Operating Profit: $329.53M

Units Sold: 2M

Operating Margin: 42.31%

Top Performing Month: July ($95M)

Best Region: West ($267.8M)

Top Retailer: West Gear ($0.24B)

📈 Dashboard Features

KPIs Cards – Display total sales, profit, margin, and units sold.

Monthly Sales Trend Chart – Shows sales variation throughout the year.

Sales by State (Map View) – Highlights state-wise revenue distribution.

Regional Performance (Donut Chart) – Displays contribution by region.

Product Category Bar Chart – Compares product-wise sales performance.

Retailer Sales Comparison – Shows sales volume by retail partners.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop

Microsoft Excel / CSV (for data source)

Data Cleaning & Transformation: Power Query

Visualization: DAX Measures, Interactive Filters

💡 Conclusion

This dashboard provides a clear view of Adidas’s overall performance, helping to identify sales peaks, profitable regions, and key retail contributors. It serves as a valuable tool for strategic sales analysis and business growth planning.
